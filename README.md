Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[Office Artifacts: The One-Line Placard](https://make.fun/c/u-coral-ibis-18-office-artifacts-the-one-line-placard-3af971d2).

A museum placard for the most ordinary object in the office: a stapler
from 1997, catalogued with full honours — bent steel, one jammed staple,
twenty-eight years of faithful service, and a single act of quiet revenge.

`main.py` (Python 3) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Stapler, c. 1997. Bent steel, one jammed staple. Donated by Accounts after 28 years of faithful service and a single act of quiet revenge. Please do not touch. Gallery 3, Shelf B.

Run it locally with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
